Changamire Dynasty of the Rozwi Empire

Origins

Changamire Dombo

Changamire Dombo I originally rose as a powerful cattle lord and provincial governor within the Mutapa state during the 17th century. Between 1684-1695 he utilized his immense wealth, building a highly disciplined elite army known as the Varozvi and rebelled against Mutapa's authority. He decisively defeated the ruling dynasty and drove the Portuguese traders entirely out of the Zimbabwean interior. Through these sweeping conquests, he founded the Rozvi Empire, establishing himself as its supreme ruler and the very first Mambo Changamire

Top Global Trade Partners

  1. Qing Dynasty

  3. Al Busaid

The Global Trade Values By Economy (1700–1830).

  1. Textiles (The Absolute #1): Indian cotton cloth, calicos, and chintz (produced heavily in Maratha and Mughal territories) along with Chinese silks were the undisputed kings of trade. They were traded by the millions, used as actual currency in East Africa, and clothed the world.
  2. Spices, Tea, and Agricultural Staples: Grain, Omani dates, Indian pepper/spices, and Chinese tea generated massive institutional wealth for empires via customs duties and sheer consumption volume.
  3. Bullion (Silver & Gold): The global thirst for silver (flooding into Qing China) and gold mined from the Southern African interior formed the monetary bedrock of the Indian Ocean network..
  4. Ivory & Precious Luxuries: Ivory occupied this elite tier. While it could never compete with the sheer tonnage of textiles or grain, it carried an astronomical profit-to-weight ratio. A single ship carrying African ivory to India or China could fund an entire merchant family's estate for generations..
  5. Porcelain and Glassware: Chinese ceramics and Indian glass beads acted as ballast and mid-tier trade goods to swap for African raw materials.
